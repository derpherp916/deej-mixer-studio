Buttons

Pass = a full press and release. HELD without touching it means the pin is shorted to GND or the switch is turned 90°.

Knobs

Pass = both ends reached (≤ 20 and ≥ 1003). Values drifting on their own mean a loose wiper, 5V or GND.
