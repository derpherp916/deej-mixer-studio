DEEJ MIXER  v2.0
================

A desk mixer: 5 volume knobs, 6 programmable buttons, and LEDs that show the volume and mute state.

EASIEST INSTALL
---------------
Double-click DeejMixer-Setup.exe and click Yes when Windows asks for permission. That's it: there
are no pages to click through, and Deej Mixer opens by itself a few seconds later. It installs the
app, adds the USB driver to Windows' driver store (Windows then loads it by itself whenever the
mixer is plugged in), creates Start-menu/desktop shortcuts and starts Deej Mixer with Windows.
Uninstall from Settings > Apps like any other program. Silent install: DeejMixer-Setup.exe /S
Choose the folder or components yourself: DeejMixer-Setup.exe /custom

QUICK START (without installing)
--------------------------------
1. Plug the mixer into the PC with a USB data cable.
2. Double-click DeejMixer.exe. The Deej Mixer window opens, and a knob icon appears in the
   taskbar notification area (bottom right, maybe under the ^ arrow).
   - If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, click "More info" > "Run anyway".
3. The mixer is found automatically. The header turns green: "Connected · COMx".
   - If it never connects, open Settings and click "Install CH340 USB driver",
     then unplug and replug the mixer.
4. Click "Install" in Settings. This keeps Deej Mixer in your Start menu
   and starts it quietly whenever you sign in.

Closing the window is fine: the mixer keeps working in the background.
Reopen it by clicking the tray icon. Right-click the tray icon to quit.

WHAT EVERYTHING DOES (DEFAULTS)
-------------------------------
Facing the mixer with the big knob on the LEFT:

  Big knob (A0) ........ Master volume. The LED ring shows the level.
  Small knob 1 (A1) .... Opera              (nearest the big knob)
  Small knob 2 (A2) .... Steam / the game you launched most recently
  Small knob 3 (A3) .... Discord
  Small knob 4 (A4) .... Spotify            (farthest)

  Opera logo button .... mute/unmute Opera
  Steam logo button .... mute/unmute the game (hold: open Steam)
  Discord logo button .. mute/unmute Discord (hold: open Discord)
  Spotify logo button .. mute/unmute Spotify (hold: open Spotify)
  Small left button .... mute/unmute everything (master)
  Small right button ... mute/unmute your microphone (the ring turns red while the mic is muted)

Each logo LED is on at full brightness, and turns OFF when its app is muted.
Everything goes dark when the master volume is muted.

All of this can be changed in the app: click any knob or button on the Device page. Profiles
(top right) let you keep several setups. Lighting sets LED colours; Firmware Studio lets you
edit, compile and upload the Arduino code itself (like Arduino IDE, built in).

HARDWARE TEST
-------------
Diagnostics -> Start test. Press every button and turn every knob all the way both ways.
Each one turns green when it passes. Use the LED patterns ("Chase" lights one LED at a time)
to check every light. Nothing is muted or changed while the test runs.

No PC needed: hold the Opera button while plugging in USB. The ring then follows whichever
knob you turn, and each logo LED lights while its button is held. Unplug to exit.

FILES
-----
  DeejMixer.exe ........ the app (keep the tools and drivers folders next to it)
  tools\ ............... AVRDUDE, used by "Upload firmware"
  drivers\ ............. WCH CH340/CH341 USB driver (ch341\ = signed package for the driver store)
  firmware\ ............ Arduino sketch (DeejMixer.ino) and compiled DeejMixer.hex
  source\ .............. full source code and build notes
  Settings and log: %APPDATA%\DeejMixer   (Settings > "Open settings folder")
